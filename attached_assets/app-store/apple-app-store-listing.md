## Description
```
Youth Dance Music helps parents, youth leaders, and DJs quickly verify whether a song is appropriate before adding it to a youth dance playlist.

Stop second-guessing songs the night before an event. Type in any song title, and our AI reviewer checks it against For the Strength of Youth standards in seconds — giving you a clear verdict, a reason why, and any specific concerns to be aware of.

HOW IT WORKS

Search any song by title and artist. The app finds it on Spotify, then our AI evaluator checks the lyrics, themes, and tone against age-appropriate standards for youth dances. You get one of three verdicts:

• Approved — safe to add to the playlist
• Review Needed — caution advised; reasoning included
• Not Recommended — flagged concerns listed clearly

FEATURES

✓ AI Song Verification — instant evaluation with full reasoning and specific concerns highlighted
✓ Popular Songs — see which songs other youth groups are vetting and approving; a growing library of pre-checked tracks
✓ Song Requests — dancers can send song requests to the DJ during an event using a dance code or QR code scan
✓ Danceability Score — see how suitable a song is for dancing, not just whether it's appropriate
✓ Dance Type — know whether a song fits contemporary, ballroom, hip-hop, or other styles

PERFECT FOR

• Youth dance coordinators and leaders preparing event playlists
• Parents who want to preview songs before approving them
• DJs managing song requests at youth events
• Anyone who wants confidence that their playlist fits the occasion

PRO ACCOUNT

Free accounts include 5 song searches per day — plenty for casual use. Upgrade to Pro for:
• Unlimited song searches
• Full access to the Popular Songs library
• Priority AI evaluation
• Subscription helps keep the app running and improving

Sign in with your Google account to get started. No separate registration required.
```

> **Privacy Policy note:** Apple requires a privacy policy URL. Because the app collects Google sign-in data and runs a subscription, you need at least a simple page covering: what data is collected (name, email, Google ID), how it's used (authentication, subscription billing), that it's not sold to third parties, and how users can request deletion (the "Delete account" button already handles this).
